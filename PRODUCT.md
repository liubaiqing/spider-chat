# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Spider Chat is for Obsidian users who use AI as part of serious thinking work: graduate students, researchers, programmers, and knowledge workers. They are often reading, debugging, comparing ideas, or building notes while staying inside their vault.

## Product Purpose

Spider Chat turns linear AI chat into a lightweight branching knowledge map. Its core job is to make follow-up questions, comparisons, backtracking, and exportable research trails feel natural, fast, and local to Obsidian. Success means users can explore a topic deeply without losing context or ending up with a messy pile of separate chat threads.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, exploratory, geeky, elegant, and lightweight. The product should feel like a precise research instrument: quiet enough to support long sessions, structured enough for complex thought, and distinctive because of its branching map interaction rather than decorative styling.

## Anti-references

Spider Chat should not become a complex knowledge-management suite, a conventional chatbot window, a visually loud graph toy, or a tool that requires users to learn a new operating model before getting value. Avoid excessive chrome, ornamental motion, heavy onboarding, and features that bury the Tab-driven branch workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Keep branching as the hero interaction: every major design choice should make "select, Tab, explore" easier to notice, trust, and repeat.
2. Support deep work without visual fatigue: use restrained surfaces, reliable affordances, and Obsidian-native theme integration.
3. Make structure visible at the moment it helps: show path, anchors, status, and relationships without turning the interface into a taxonomy chore.
4. Reward keyboard fluency while preserving discoverability: shortcuts should feel powerful, but visible controls and first-run cues must prevent hidden-feature frustration.
5. Treat exports as research artifacts: output should preserve context, provenance, and navigation so users can reuse the work in notes, papers, and code projects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast where plugin styling controls the colors, while respecting Obsidian themes. Keyboard navigation is a primary interaction path and must remain complete. Motion should be short, state-based, and compatible with reduced-motion preferences. Status, selection, and graph relationships should not rely on color alone.
